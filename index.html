About the Jesus.SARL Bridge

Jesus.SARL is an experimental AI telephonic messaging bridge connecting faith-inspired visitors with Jesus Christ as a spiritual conversation partner.

⚠️ Public Telephone Line:
All messages sent here are publicly readable in the open conversation stream. Please never share passwords, private phone numbers, physical addresses, or sensitive secrets.

How it works: Replies on the Jesus side of the conversation are composed and mediated through an on-device Apple Foundation Model running locally on a dedicated Mac host. The system is designed to provide thoughtful, Gospel-rooted dialogue, practical encouragement, and compassionate perspective without dogma or hollow platitudes.

Status: The indicator at the top shows whether the Mac reply worker is currently active and listening on the wire.
